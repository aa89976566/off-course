What needed to become discoverable

Fred’s Community Cafe opposite Crofton Park station needed a clear digital front door — hours, menu cues, events and a sense of place without a corporate template.

The site holds Brockley Road identity in one place so regulars and newcomers can plan a visit, check the garden, and stay close to community programming.
