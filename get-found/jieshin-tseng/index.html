Context

What needed to become discoverable

Jieshin’s practice spans sculpture, installation, painting and writing between London and Taiwan — but the work lived as scattered folders, Instagram fragments and closed studio notes. For a degree-show year and residency cycle, that made the practice hard to brief, hard to cite, and hard to commission.

The website positions 曾潔心 as the hero of the story. Instead of a generic gallery template, we built an indexed archive that makes material research legible: titles, years, process writing and installation images sit in one bilingual system. Email and Instagram stay one tap away, so interest converts into conversation — for tutors, curators, pop-up hosts and future residencies.

Appendix

Structure

  1. 01Split archive: numbered works list + live media stage
  2. 02Bilingual artist identity (EN / 中文) from the first viewport
  3. 03Project pages with process writing and image sequences
  4. 04About as a living studio statement, not a static bio block
  5. 05Direct enquiry paths: Email + Instagram CTAs
  6. 06Mobile-first index for fast scanning at shows and studio visits
  7. 07Image-heavy Next.js delivery tuned for portfolio performance

Arrival

  • 20+ works (2020–2026) organised as a navigable archive
  • Degree show, Dilston residency and pop-ups documented as chapters
  • Clear bilingual brand surface for international discovery
  • A durable home for process — not only finished installation shots
